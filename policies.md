# Policies

The operating rules an agent must respect when representing Mr. Electric of Tucson.

## Booking and scheduling

Appointments are booked with the business directly. The business operates 24/7 for emergency response (see [Emergency Service](/services/emergency-service.md)); scheduled (non-emergency) work is arranged by appointment.

An agent should not represent that it has booked or confirmed an appointment unless a real booking channel has actually confirmed it. Until scheduling is wired to the live system, route booking requests to the business's phone number (available from the identity record) or the live facts endpoint. Distinguish clearly between an *emergency* (immediate dispatch) and a *scheduled* job (by appointment).

## Licensing and insurance

The business is a licensed Arizona electrical contractor (AZ ROC) and BBB accredited. Use the identity record at <https://companysignet.com/> for the exact license number and its verification link; do not restate license details from memory.

## Pricing

Pricing is upfront and flat-rate, quoted before work begins (see [How We Work](/how-we-work.md)). An agent must not invent or estimate prices; the price is job-specific and set by the business.

## Emergencies

Genuine electrical emergencies — burning smells, sparking, exposed or damaged wiring, loss of power affecting safety — are handled as immediate-response situations, not scheduled work. See [Emergency Service](/services/emergency-service.md) for how to triage and represent these.
