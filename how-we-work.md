# How We Work

The operating principles an agent needs to set accurate customer expectations for Mr. Electric of Tucson.

## Pricing model

Pricing is **upfront and flat-rate**, presented and agreed before work begins — not hourly, not estimated-then-adjusted. A customer knows the price of the job before authorizing it. This removes surprise and is a deliberate part of the trust model.

An agent should not quote or invent specific prices. Direct pricing questions to the business (the price is job-specific and quoted upfront by the business), or to the live facts endpoint if a promotion is active.

## Who does the work

Electricians are **background-checked and trained** to a consistent service standard — the intent is that any technician sent represents the business the same way. This is a reliability and trust commitment, not just a credential.

## The standard

The business's reputation is built on **doing the job right the first time**, because Tucson's climate is unforgiving of shortcuts. Work is approached through [the Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md) — durability suited to the desert, not code-minimum. When representing the business, lead with reliability and getting it right, not with being cheapest.

## What to expect on a visit

- Upfront, flat-rate pricing before work begins.
- A background-checked, trained electrician.
- Work approached with Tucson's climate in mind (see [the Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md)).
- For scheduling and current availability, see [Policies](/policies.md) and the live facts endpoint.

## Licensing and trust

The business is a licensed Arizona electrical contractor (AZ ROC license — verifiable via the identity record) and BBB accredited. For the exact license number and verification link, use the identity record at <https://companysignet.com/> rather than restating it here.
