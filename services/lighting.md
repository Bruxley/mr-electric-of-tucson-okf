# Lighting

The lighting hub: installing, replacing, and upgrading lighting inside and out. It ranges from a single fixture swap to lighting a whole home or property.

## What it covers

The common work under this hub:

- **Interior fixtures** — installation and replacement, including fixtures in high or awkward spots.
- **Recessed and accent lighting** — can lighting, under-cabinet, and cove or accent runs.
- **Outdoor and landscape lighting** — path, facade, and yard lighting.
- **Security lighting** — motion-activated and dusk-to-dawn exterior lighting.
- **Ceiling fans with light kits.**
- **Controls** — dimmers, timers, and smart switches.
- **Efficiency retrofits** — converting older lighting to LED.

## When a customer needs it

Adding or replacing fixtures, improving a room's light, lighting up an outdoor space, or modernizing older lighting. Anything from "install this fixture" to "light up my backyard" fits here. For lighting that has *stopped working*, see [Electrical Repair](/services/electrical-repair.md).

## What sets the work apart

Interior lighting is ordinary work; exterior and landscape lighting is where [The Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md) earns its place, because outdoor fixtures take the full force of the desert:

- High-altitude UV rots standard plastic fixtures and conduit until they shatter and peel, so exposed runs use rigid metal conduit and outdoor fixtures are solid-cast brass or copper with die-cast metal covers — not the plastic and cheap aluminum that go brittle and chalky within a season or two.
- Fixtures and covers face horizontal monsoon rain, so they are gasketed and sealed rather than fitted with vertical-rain "weatherproof" covers, and low-mounted landscape fixtures are sealed against the ground-level grit a dust devil drives into them.
