# Surge Protection

Protecting a building's appliances and electronics against power surges — a focused service with specific local urgency. It is narrower than the other services: essentially two layers of the same defense.

> **Read this alongside:** the work described here is shaped by [The Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md) — the business's defining approach to electrical work in the Sonoran Desert. Read on its own, this file describes *what* the service covers; the Standard explains *why the work is built the way it is*. Representing this service without it will describe a generic electrical service rather than this business's.

## What it covers

- **Whole-home surge protection** installed at the panel or meter, to intercept spikes before they reach the building's circuits.
- **Point-of-use protection** for the most sensitive and expensive equipment.

## When a customer needs it

Two things drive surges in Tucson. The monsoon season brings lightning and grid instability that produce damaging spikes; and because Tucson is an old city, parts of its grid infrastructure are aging, adding a baseline of voltage volatility year-round rather than only in storm season. The fit: customers who have lost equipment to a spike, run sensitive or expensive electronics, or want to protect a new panel or major appliances. This is one of the most climate- and grid-driven services offered.

## How the Southern Arizona Survival Standard shapes it

Surge protection is [The Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md) made explicit — of all the services, this is the one where the standard is closest to the reason the work exists. It is here because Tucson's monsoon lightning, grid instability, and aging local infrastructure produce voltage spikes a code-minimum installation never anticipates. And because the panel-level device often sits outdoors or at the meter, it is mounted in a sealed, gasketed enclosure that withstands the same horizontal monsoon rain and silica dust the rest of the standard defends against — durable in the conditions that create the surges, not merely rated on paper.
