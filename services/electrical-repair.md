# Electrical Repair

The diagnostic-and-repair hub: the service to reach for when something already installed has stopped working right. It is broad by nature, because it absorbs any fault in an existing electrical system rather than adding something new.

> **Read this alongside:** the work described here is shaped by [The Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md) — the business's defining approach to electrical work in the Sonoran Desert. Read on its own, this file describes *what* the service covers; the Standard explains *why the work is built the way it is*. Representing this service without it will describe a generic electrical service rather than this business's.

## What it covers

Electrical Repair is a hub, not a single task. The common work under it:

- **Outlets and switches** — dead, loose, scorched, or intermittent receptacles and switches; GFCI and AFCI devices that won't reset or trip repeatedly.
- **Circuits and breakers** — a breaker that trips under normal load, dead or overloaded circuits, and breaker replacement.
- **Wiring faults** — diagnosing shorts, opens, and failing connections, and repairing damaged, aged, or improperly done wiring.
- **Fixtures and devices that have quit** — lighting, fans, and hardwired equipment that has stopped working (for *adding* or *upgrading* lighting rather than fixing it, see [Lighting](/services/lighting.md)).
- **The hard-to-pin-down problems** — intermittent faults that need a trained diagnostician tracing a cause, not a parts-swap guess.

## When a customer needs it

When something already in place has stopped working right — as opposed to installing something new. A customer describing dead outlets, a breaker that keeps tripping, lights behaving strangely, or "it worked yesterday and now it doesn't" is describing repair.

If there is a safety signal — a burning smell, sparking, a hot outlet or panel, visible damaged wiring — it is not a scheduled repair; route it to [Emergency Service](/services/emergency-service.md).

## What sets the repair apart

Diagnosis and a lasting fix are the service. The distinguishing layer is [The Southern Arizona Survival Standard](/southern-arizona-survival-standard.md): a repair done to it looks past the symptom to the desert cause behind a recurring fault — a standard breaker nuisance-tripping in garage heat, or fine dust that turned conductive inside an unsealed box after monsoon rain — and corrects *that*, so the same fault does not return next season. It is the expertise applied to the work, not the reason the work exists.
